# Original concept and expansion record

Source: “Chaotic Co-op Extraction Game,” conversation ID `6a6b862b-36b4-83ea-bea0-246e0ecddb20`, read on 14 September 2026.

The user wanted an HTML game that could later reach Steam: a group of scientists visits planets, moons, asteroids, and comets, harvests valuable materials using tools that may overheat or break, and returns as much cargo as possible before a deadline. Materials differ in mass and value. The intended experience is silly, chaotic, cooperative, and easy to play, with exaggerated low-gravity movement.

Earlier discussion proposed the working title Moon Goons, 1–4 players, physical cargo, flexible tool roles, a ship hub, research unlocks, seven destination ideas, and a small first playable. Those suggestions were recovered as design material, not proof of implemented features. No old code or assets were available in the referenced conversation.

The new bible turns that premise into explicit rules and separates prototype content from future possibilities. It replaces ambiguous punishment and currency ideas with free starter equipment, capped rescue fees, credits/RP/discoveries, and a readable recovery model. All additions remain editable through evidence from playtesting.
